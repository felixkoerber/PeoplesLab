## Introduction  
The **PeopleLab** is an interdisciplinary research group founded and led by **Dr. Alessandro Tavano**.  
Our mission is to understand how **temporal dynamics** shape **auditory perception and cognition**.  
By integrating neuroscience, psychology, and computational modeling, the lab investigates how the brain predicts, adapts to, and learns from time-based regularities in sound.

---

## Mission  
- Explore how the brain encodes *when* events occur within complex auditory streams.  
- Combine experimental neuroscience (EEG, MEG, eye-tracking, ECG, respiration) with **computational and machine-learning approaches**.  
- Foster a truly interdisciplinary space uniting neuroscientists, psychologists, and computer scientists.  
- Illuminate how **time** governs **perception, language, and cognition**.

---

## Research Themes  

### 1. Temporal Expectations & Auditory Encoding  
- Investigating how **temporal predictions** (regularity, hazard functions) interact with **content predictions** (features, semantics).  
- Studying how **temporal noise**—irregular onset patterns—modulates sensory encoding and perceptual accuracy.  
- Relevant work: *Tavano et al., 2019 – Beta power encodes contextual estimates of temporal event probability.*

### 2. Neural Oscillations & Auditory Streams  
- Examining how oscillatory dynamics (delta, theta, alpha, beta) synchronize to **speech and music rhythms**.  
- Differentiating the roles of **phase coherence** and **power** in encoding attention to auditory sequences.  
- Relevant work: *Tavano & Poeppel, 2019 – A division of labour between power and phase coherence in encoding attention.*

### 3. Naturalistic Auditory-Visual Contexts  
- Moving beyond isolated tones to **ecologically valid stimuli** such as film soundtracks and narratives.  
- Measuring **neural synchrony** across participants during shared auditory experiences.  
- Linking naturalistic auditory input with perception, attention, and emotional engagement.

### 4. Computational Modeling & Prediction Frameworks  
- Applying **AI and machine-learning** techniques to model prediction, adaptation, and timing in auditory processing.  
- Developing computational frameworks for neural oscillations and temporal integration.

### 5. Neurocognitive Applications  
- Connecting subcortical and cortical timing mechanisms to higher-order cognition.  
- Translating auditory research to **language comprehension**, **clinical assessment**, and **neuro-adaptive interfaces**.

---

## Methods & Tools  
- **Human electrophysiology:** EEG, MEG.  
- **Peripheral physiology:** ECG, respiration, and eye-tracking.  
- **Behavioral psychophysics:** auditory discrimination and prediction tasks.  
- **Computational analysis:** time-series modeling, oscillatory signal decomposition, and machine-learning classification.  
- **Naturalistic paradigms:** movie watching, multisensory synchrony, and cross-participant neural coupling.
